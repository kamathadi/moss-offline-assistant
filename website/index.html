YOUR TEXT DOES NOT LEAVE THIS LAPTOP

Private by design.
Ready offline.

Moss is a small helper for writing. It can check and improve text you select, suggest word choices, summarize, and rephrase.

It runs MossAI on your computer. Moss does not send your writing to the cloud or store it. Your preferences are saved locally so Moss remembers which tools and shortcuts you chose.

Install once, then turn Wi-Fi off: the writing tools still work. No account is needed.
